06Design Rules設計原則

Eight design rules八條設計原則

The doctrine behind every pixel — built so anomalies are detected in milliseconds, not minutes.每個像素背後的原則 — 讓異常以毫秒級而非分鐘級被察覺。

R1Anomalies scream, normalcy whispers異常搶眼，正常低調

Exactly one card may glow red — this month, alcohol rule-breaks. Everything healthy stays muted so the anomaly owns the eye.只容一張卡發紅光 — 本月是酒類違規。健康的指標保持低調，讓異常獨佔視線。

Applied: 1 red card among 8 · 2 alerts among 6 signals應用：8卡中1紅 · 6個訊號中2個告警

R2The 0.5-second rule半秒法則

Critical health sits top-left with a one-line verdict, readable at a glance — from three metres away on a wall screen.關鍵健康狀態置於左上，附一句結論 — 牆屏三米外一眼可讀。

Applied: “Steady — 2 items need attention” is read first應用：「平穩 — 兩項需關注」最先被讀到

R3Context or it doesn't ship無上下文，不上線

Every metric carries a baseline or target plus a 12-month sparkline. A number without context is banned.每項指標必附基準或目標，加上12個月迷你走勢圖。禁止孤立數字。

Applied: 8 / 8 KPI cards carry spark + target tick應用：8 / 8 張卡均有走勢＋目標刻度

R4Gauges vetoed否決速度計圖表

Speedometer gauges burn space and hide history. Bullet graphs show progress against target; sparklines show direction.速度計圖表浪費空間且隱藏歷史。子彈圖顯示對目標進度；走勢圖顯示方向。

Applied: inspections 95% bullet · prevalence vs 7.8% tick應用：巡查95%子彈圖 · 吸煙率對7.8%刻度

R5Never colour alone絕不單靠顏色

Every status pairs colour with a shape and a word, so colour-blind readers and monochrome prints lose nothing.每個狀態以形狀＋文字配對顏色，色弱讀者與黑白列印同樣無損。

Applied: ✓ on track · ▲ watch · ⬣ act now · ○ near · — steady應用：✓ 達標 · ▲ 觀察 · ⬣ 立即行動 · ○ 接近 · — 平穩

R6Group alerts, kill fatigue告警分組，防疲勞

Show only what demands action; collapse minor advisories into one quiet, expandable row.只顯示需要行動的告警；輕微提示收進一行可展開的安靜列。

Applied: 2 decision alerts + 4 advisories grouped應用：2個決策告警＋4個提示分組

R7Honest freshness時效標示要誠實

A monthly dashboard must never fake a live pulse. Stamp the data month and the next update instead.每月儀表板絕不假裝實時跳動。改為標示數據月份及下次更新日期。

Applied: “Data Oct 2026 · Monthly · Next 17 Nov 2026”應用：「數據2026年10月 · 每月 · 下次11月17日」

R8Plain words first顯淺用語優先

Executives read outcomes, not jargon: FPN becomes “smoking tickets”; SLA becomes “replies within 2 days”.高層讀的是成果，不是術語：FPN 變成「吸煙告票」；SLA 變成「兩天內回覆」。

Applied: full glossary in section 09應用：完整對照表見第09節
